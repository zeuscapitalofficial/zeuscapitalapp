# Zeus Capital

## Vision

Zeus Capital is a modern cryptocurrency brokerage and mining platform that enables users to manage digital assets, monitor mining operations, and grow their portfolios through a secure, intuitive, and premium experience.

The platform combines portfolio management, mining, market analytics, and account management into one unified ecosystem.

---

# Product Principles

The platform should always prioritize:

- Simplicity
- Trust
- Security
- Transparency
- Performance
- Accessibility
- Long-term maintainability

# Target Audience

## Individual Investors

Users who want to:

- Track crypto assets
- Monitor mining rewards
- Manage wallets
- View portfolio analytics

---

## Mining Customers

Users who purchase or manage mining plans.

Features include:

- Mining dashboard
- Rewards
- Payouts
- Mining history
- Performance monitoring

---

## Administrators

Internal staff responsible for:

- User management
- KYC
- Mining plans
- Coin management
- Transactions
- Platform settings
